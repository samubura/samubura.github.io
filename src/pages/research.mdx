### Ph.D. thesis

[**Digital Twins Ecosystems for Intelligent Applications in Healthcare**](https://doi.org/10.48676/unibo/amsdottorato/12963)<br/>
University of Bologna, defended March 2026. Advisor: Prof. Alessandro Ricci, co-advisor:
Prof. Mirko Viroli.

Digitalising healthcare promises better efficiency, quality and traceability of care, but the
software needed to get there has to bring together many heterogeneous systems. The thesis studies
the engineering of *Digital Twin Ecosystems*: networks of heterogeneous digital twins linked by
dynamic relationships, proposed as an abstraction layer on which intelligent applications can be
built. It introduces modular, interoperable architectures for digital twins and semantic
descriptions that support their operation and integration. Building on the Web and the Web of
Things, it presents the Hypermedia Web of Digital Twins (HWoDT), a prototype platform for
implementing such ecosystems, and explores BDI multi-agent systems as a way to build reliable
intelligent applications on top of them. The approach is validated in healthcare scenarios.
