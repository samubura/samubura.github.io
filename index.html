Research

My Ph.D. thesis, Digital Twins Ecosystems for Intelligent Applications in Healthcare (University of Bologna, 2025, advised by Prof. Alessandro Ricci), asked how independently-developed digital twins can be composed into ecosystems that software agents can actually reason about. I keep pulling on that thread.

Digital Twin Ecosystems

Hypermedia and Linked Data as the interoperability layer between digital twins — self-describing twins that agents can discover and compose at runtime, across the edge-cloud continuum.

Engineering Multi-Agent Systems

BDI agent programming languages and frameworks: concurrency models, explainability, anticipatory reasoning, and the knowledge-level framing of the Agents & Artifacts model.

Web of Things & Hypermedia MAS

Agents that use the Web as their environment — affordance discovery, domain-expert configuration of industrial hypermedia MAS, and agent embodiment in hypermedia spaces.

Applications

Healthcare (operating-room management, oncologic pharmaceutical supply chains), smart manufacturing and Industry 4.0, and urban digital twins.
